privacy and terms

updated 3 November 2026

otto has no account system and no server of its own. your vault never leaves your Mac, and nothing about you is sold or shared.

privacy

the app

  • the vault (a KeePass file), its backups and the activity log are in your Library folder, and the vault's key is in your Mac's keychain. none of it is sent anywhere. the activity log says what agents asked for and how it went, never the secrets otto keeps.
  • to show a login's site icon, otto gets the icon from that site and keeps a copy on your Mac. the site sees a request from your Mac and nothing else.
  • every 24 hours, unless you turn it off in settings, otto checks otto.alfaoz.dev for a new version. updates are handled by Sparkle, and the check says only which version of otto you have.
  • if you turn on remote, your phone connects to your Mac through your own Tailscale network, and only devices on your Tailscale account can reach it. Tailscale's own privacy policy covers Tailscale.
  • if an agent asks otto to read a 2FA setup QR code from your screen, otto takes one picture of it, reads the code, and throws the picture away without saving it.
  • agents you connect get what you let them have: results like “filled”, 6-digit codes, and the values you hand to programs with otto run. what an agent, and the AI service behind it, does with those is up to them. otto does its best to keep things safe, but nothing is guaranteed on the agent's side.

the Chrome extension

  • it only talks to otto on your own Mac, through Chrome's native messaging. it keeps no history and sends nothing over the network.
  • when an agent asks otto to fill a login, a 2FA code or a card, the extension looks at your tabs' addresses to find the one on the right site, and fills the fields on that page.
  • when an agent asks otto to read a 2FA setup QR code, the extension takes a picture of that tab. otto reads the code from it on your Mac and throws the picture away without saving it.
  • what it does with your data follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

this website

  • this website has no cookies and no analytics, and neither does otto. the site loads nothing from anywhere else.
  • otto.alfaoz.dev keeps standard access logs (IP address, time, the page or file, the browser) for about two weeks, to keep it running. otto's update checks and downloads are in them too.
